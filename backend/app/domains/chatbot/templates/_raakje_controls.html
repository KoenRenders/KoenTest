{% import "_macros.html" as ui %} {# #1075: the controls of Raakje, in one place. Raakje appears on four surfaces — the public widget, `/raakje`, the reporting assistant and the record overlays — and Koen's rule for them is one sentence: *everywhere the same; the only difference is the security on the public one.* The controls are the part that must not differ: the growing input field, Enter sends and Shift+Enter breaks the line, the microphone, and reading answers aloud. Before this file each surface carried its own copy of that row, and the overlay had silently lost the microphone and the read-aloud toggle — exactly the drift a copy invites. A new Raakje surface calls these macros and inherits the controls; it does not copy them (design-system §2.11). Like `_raakje_ballon.html`, deliberately NOT in `_macros.html`: a kit macro would need a place on `/admin/design-system`, and that is a separate decision (#791). This stays inside the chatbot domain and is imported from there. The toolset — what Raakje may look up — is not in here. It is bound in the route (`run_public_chat` versus the assistant dispatcher of the reporting domain) and guarded by `tests/test_public_tool_boundary_gate.py`. #} {# The input row: the question field, the microphone and the send button. `id` must be unique on the page — the microphone addresses the field by it (`data-stt-target`), and the widget and `/raakje` never share a page with a second Raakje. `stt_mode` comes from the configuration and decides whether the browser listens itself or our Voxtral proxy does; without a value stt.js falls back to the browser. `icoon_verzenden` is the ONE difference between the surfaces: the public widget sends with a paper-plane icon (it is 20rem wide), the back-office surfaces with the word *Vraag*.
