{# The question block of a form — ONE place (#1380). The form's own page (`formulier.html`) and every page that asks a form's questions render this: the registration page and the board's registration page (`_inschrijf_velden.html`), the answer link of a registration (the form's own page) and the correction on the registration detail. Until #1380 the registration built the frame around the fields again, and every improvement of the form's own page stayed behind. Two places for one thing (CLAUDE.md). CR-11 pilot B (#1589; `docs/design-system-end-state.md` §2.6): the cards are the kit's section cards — one per section of the form, and one for the fields in no section — with the section's title as the card's head (Inter 16 px semibold), 32 px between cards and the questions on the form grid, 12 px apart. One long page: no steps. Two macros: - `vragen_kop` — the form's title and description as a heading, for a page that shows the block INSIDE something of its own (the registration detail in the back office). The form's own page puts its name in a name card, and the registration page asks under its own section "Vragen bij de inschrijving". - `vragen_kaarten` — the cards. Parameters of `vragen_kaarten`: - `vraag_fout` — the id of the question a refusal names, marked, where the page is redrawn with the refusal (the registration detail). A page with the kit's message line gets the mark from `record-form.js` instead; - `cards_attrs` — attributes on the wrapper of the cards (the registration's `x-show`), empty on the form's own page; - `path` — the form branches: each card carries its place (`data-step`) and is shown only while it lies on the path the answers lead along (`formulier.html`, `formPath`).
