Until #1380 the registration built the frame around the fields again, and every improvement of the form's own page stayed behind. Two places for one thing (CLAUDE.md). CR-11 pilot B (#1589; `docs/design-system-end-state.md` §2.6): the cards are the kit's section cards — one per section of the form, and one for the fields in no section — with the section's title as the card's head (Inter 16 px semibold), 16 px between cards (#1899, as the form flow) and the questions on the form grid, 12 px apart. One long page: no steps. One macro, `vragen_kaarten` — the cards. (`vragen_kop`, the form's title and description as a heading for the registration detail in the back office, left with that detail's own form: the record page of a registration, #2127, asks the questions under the cards' own `title`.)
