Each person is a composite row that folds: the main member open, the others closed; no handle, because the persons of a household have no order the visitor sets. Each person carries their e-mail addresses as a child group with the *hoofdadres* tag — which may be removed like any other address, and then nothing takes its place (Koen, 27 September 2026; #1603). Nothing here saves on its own: the rows are part of the page's one form and its one bar. The field names are the contract of `mdm.household_form`. A person added in the page chooses partner or child (#1603), on both pages. The page asks the server which of the two the one rule gives them (`default_relation`: a partner while the household has none, a child after that) — the rule stays in one place.
